- The garage is called « Garage Carrosserie de la Plaine » everywhere it is named (`garage.name`), as the garage asked. « Garage de la Plaine » and the logo's hyphenated « Garage-Carrosserie » are kept only as alternate names for search (`alternateNames`).
- Keep French garage content in a shared source module, with individual TanStack content routes and reusable site sections; this keeps verified facts consistent across all pages.
- Use Autolina as the live inventory source and external listing destination, never a local stock database; stock must stay owned by the dealer's feed. The homepage preview reads the dealer's Autolina listing page at request time (`src/lib/autolina.ts`, `src/lib/stock.functions.ts`) and falls back to a plain stock link; never hard-code cars or prices.
- The menu follows the visitor's need (`menu` in `site-shell.tsx`): Accueil (the garage asked to keep it), Véhicules ▾ (Suzuki neuves /suzuki, Stock neuf et occasion /vehicules, Vendre ou faire reprendre /reprise), Carrosserie (/carrosserie), Atelier (/atelier), Le garage ▾ (L’entreprise, Station-service, Paiement WIR), Contact, then the red Rendez-vous button; the footer lists the same pages. /services no longer exists: it and the old garageplaine.ch addresses redirect (`src/lib/legacy-urls.ts`). /carrosserie holds `BodyworkSection`, /atelier holds `MechanicsSection` (built like the body shop on a light band: key figures, the workshop tour over the real photo with stops in `workshopTour`, one card per service from `mechanicalCards`, Jérôme's card), each with its own FAQ set and `ContactBand`. /vehicules opens with three entry tabs (Suzuki neuves, the stock, Vendre ma voiture). Tiago Bessa (service vente) is the contact on every car-related page.
- Keep the supplied logo and actual garage photographs as image files in `src/assets/` (imported directly, no Lovable asset pointers), and distinguish generated editorial imagery from real inventory imagery; avoid misrepresenting the business. Page headers are `PageIntro` with an `image` (full-width photo, navy veil, white title, two actions; `compact` on form pages). Use the garage's own photos (`photos` in `sections.tsx`) and never the same photo twice on one page; where the garage has none, a free-licence stock photo (`stockPhotos`, Pexels licence) or a generated image, always with `illustration: true` so it is labelled « Visuel d’illustration ». Record every non-garage image in `src/assets/CREDITS.md`; never suzuki.ch images or non-commercial licences.
- The site is hosted on Netlify: `vite.config.ts` pins nitro's `netlify` preset and `netlify.toml` holds the build settings; don't reintroduce Lovable- or Cloudflare-hosted dependencies.
- Netlify deploys cost credits: group pushes to the connected branch, one or two a day at most (commit locally in between); `netlify.toml`'s `ignore` skips builds for docs- and test-only changes.
- The domain garageplaine.ch (DNS and email) is at Infomaniak. At launch, only the website records change (`garageplaine.ch` and `www`, to Netlify); never touch the email settings (MX, SPF, DKIM and the like).
- Every form (contact, `/reprise`, the `/rendez-vous` planner) sends to the garage's console on the Lemon platform through `src/components/garage/platform.tsx` (tenant `garage-plaine`, `plateforme` in `src/lib/site.ts`, client `src/lib/lemon.ts` copied unchanged from lemon-site-modele), never Netlify Forms, a separate database or a prepared email. A form says "sent" only on `201 { ok: true }`; contract v1 has no field for the trade or for photos, so the trade is the message's first line and reprise photos go by email after sending, until the platform accepts them. The reprise message lists the vehicle in AutoScout24 order (`src/lib/reprise.ts`). The platform's origin is in `connect-src`: security headers live in `src/lib/security-headers.ts`, sent by `src/server.ts` (Netlify does not apply header files to server-rendered pages) and copied in `netlify.toml` and `public/_headers` (a test keeps them identical).
- The rendez-vous planner (`/rendez-vous`) is a request, not a booking: days come from the published hours (`src/lib/appointment.ts`), and the garage confirms. Keep calls to action that ask for an appointment pointing to it, with `?besoin=` set to the trade.
- Every page sets its head with `pageHead` from `src/lib/seo.ts` (title, description, canonical, share image, JSON-LD built from `garage.ts`); the site address comes from Netlify's `URL` at build time (`src/lib/site.ts`). `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and `llms.txt` are generated by server routes from the same facts, FAQ answers live in `faqs` (one set per page, never repeated), and old garageplaine.ch addresses are redirected in `src/lib/legacy-urls.ts`.
- Every section sits on one surface, PAPER (`--background`), TINT (`--surface-tint`), SAGE (`--secondary`) or INK (`--ink-band`), and neighbouring sections never share one; spacing is `--band-y` and h2 sizes `--h2-band` (the "Section rhythm and contrast" block at the end of `src/styles.css`). Separate sections by contrast, not by 1px lines.
- The full contact form lives only on /contact; every other page closes with `ContactBand` (right person first, then an appointment or a message). Every person is reached the same way, with `ContactActions`: call, WhatsApp, e-mail (the garage's WhatsApp is Tiago's mobile until the workshop and body shop give theirs).
- Phones come first: text sizes below 16px go through the `--fs-*` tokens in `src/styles.css` (they grow on screens ≤640px), form fields stay at 16px so iOS doesn't zoom, and anything tappable is at least 44px. Fonts are self-hosted in `public/fonts/` and preloaded; the hero uses a responsive `srcSet`. French text puts a no-break space (U+00A0) before `?`, `!`, `:`, `;` and inside « », except in composed email bodies.
- The Suzuki page shows the S-Cross in 3D (`SuzukiSpin`; scene in `src/lib/spin-scene.ts`, file setup, material finishes and detail points in `src/lib/s-cross-3d.ts`, gzipped model in `public/models/`, poster renders in `src/assets/`). The model is a free CC BY 4.0 file credited on the page, not a Suzuki asset: keep the credit, and never use renders or photos taken from suzuki.ch. Its figures and the range come from Suzuki Suisse's price list (`sCross`, `suzukiRange` in `garage.ts`), without prices. three.js loads only on that page; while `available` is false the hero shows the showroom photo instead.
